# How to prepare for and take the GRE

days_needed: 90

The GRE General Test is asked for by Fulbright and many US graduate programmes. It tests verbal reasoning, quantitative reasoning (maths) and analytical writing.

## Book the test
1. Create an ETS account and book the **GRE General Test** at a test centre in Pakistan or at home.
2. Use your **passport** name exactly.
3. Check the current fee on the ETS site before paying.
4. Official scores arrive about 8 to 10 days after the test.

## Prepare for free (8 to 12 weeks)
- Start with the two free **POWERPREP** practice tests from ETS to get a baseline.
- Maths is school-level (algebra, geometry, data), but questions are tricky. Practise a set every day.
- Build vocabulary in context by reading English newspapers and journals, not by memorising long lists.
- Practise the "Analyze an Issue" essay with a 30-minute timer.

## Tips
- Scores are valid for 5 years, so taking it early is fine.
- You can retake it after 21 days if you need a higher score.

**Typical time:** 2 to 3 months of preparation plus about 10 days for scores.
