# How to get an income certificate

days_needed: 14

An income certificate states your family's monthly income. Most need-based scholarships in Pakistan ask for it.

## Where to go
- **Salaried parent:** the employer's HR or accounts office issues a salary certificate on letterhead.
- **Self-employed or daily wage:** the local Union Council, Tehsil office, or a Grade 17+ gazetted officer issues an affidavit-based income certificate.
- Some universities accept an affidavit on PKR 100 stamp paper attested by an oath commissioner.

## What to bring
1. Father's or guardian's CNIC copy
2. Your CNIC or B-Form copy
3. Latest utility bill (proof of address)
4. Salary slip, if any

## Steps
1. Ask the scholarship office which format they accept; many post a sample form.
2. Fill the form and get it signed and stamped.
3. Keep two attested photocopies. Scholarships often ask for the original at verification.

**Typical time:** 3 to 14 days. Start early because offices close around public holidays.
