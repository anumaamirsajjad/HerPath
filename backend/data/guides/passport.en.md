# How to get a passport

days_needed: 30

Every scholarship abroad needs a valid passport, usually with at least 6 months left before it expires. Apply before you apply for the scholarship, not after.

## Where to go
Any Regional Passport Office, or online through the Directorate General of Immigration and Passports e-services portal (onlinemrp.dgip.gov.pk) if you have a CNIC.

## What to bring
1. Original CNIC (or B-Form with a parent's CNIC if you are under 18)
2. Copy of CNIC
3. Fee receipt from a National Bank branch or paid online (normal 10-year passport is about PKR 4,500; urgent is about PKR 7,500)
4. For students, no NOC is needed

## Steps
1. Pay the fee and keep the receipt.
2. Visit the office with your token, or complete the online form and upload a photograph.
3. Photograph, fingerprints and a short interview are done at the office.
4. Collect the passport or receive it by courier.

**Typical time:** normal 10 to 21 working days; urgent 4 to 7 working days. Plan 30 days to be safe.
