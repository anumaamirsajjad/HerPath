# How to reach 16 years of education

days_needed: 730

Most Master's scholarships, in Pakistan and abroad, ask for 16 years of education. A 2-year BA or BSc (or an Associate Degree) gives you 14 years.

## Your options
- **Continue into a BS:** Associate Degree holders can usually join a 4-year BS programme in the 5th semester and finish in about 2 more years.
- **A 2-year Master's (MA/MSc/MCom):** where still offered, this brings you to 16 years.
- **Check equivalence:** if you studied abroad, IBCC or HEC can tell you how many years your degree counts as.

## Plan it
1. Ask universities near you which bridging route they accept for your degree.
2. While you study, build the rest of your profile: English test, recommendation letters and work or volunteering.
3. Keep your CGPA up. Many scholarships want 3.0 or more.

## Good to know
Need-based scholarships (HEC Need-Based, provincial endowment funds) can help pay for the extra 2 years. Check HerPath for undergraduate scholarships while you complete your degree.

**Typical time:** about 2 years.
