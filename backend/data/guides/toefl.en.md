# How to prepare for and take TOEFL iBT

days_needed: 60

TOEFL iBT is accepted by most universities in the USA and many scholarships worldwide. It is run by ETS.

## Book the test
1. Create an account on the ETS TOEFL website and book **TOEFL iBT**, either at a test centre in Pakistan or the Home Edition on your own computer.
2. Your name must match your **passport** exactly.
3. Check the current fee on the ETS site before paying.
4. Scores are usually online about a week after the test.

## Prepare for free (6 to 8 weeks)
- Take the free official practice test on the ETS website to find your starting score.
- The test has four sections: Reading, Listening, Speaking and Writing, all on a computer.
- Speaking is recorded, not face to face. Practise answering in 45 to 60 seconds with a timer.
- Type your practice essays; you will type on test day too.

## Tips
- Scholarships usually ask for 79 to 100 out of 120. Check the exact number.
- Scores are valid for 2 years.
- If a scholarship accepts either IELTS or TOEFL, take the one whose practice test you score better on.

**Typical time:** 6 to 8 weeks of preparation plus about 1 week for scores.
