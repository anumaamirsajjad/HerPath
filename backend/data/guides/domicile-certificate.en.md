# How to get a domicile certificate

days_needed: 14

A domicile certificate proves which district and province you belong to. Provincial scholarships and government quotas depend on it.

## Where to go
The District Coordination Officer (DCO) or Deputy Commissioner (DC) office of your district. In Punjab and KP you can also apply through the provincial e-services portal and pick up the certificate later.

## What to bring
1. Your CNIC or B-Form (original and copy)
2. Father's CNIC copy and, if he has one, his domicile copy
3. Two passport-size photographs
4. Proof of residence: utility bill, rent agreement, or property paper
5. Matric certificate
6. Application form from the DC office (or printed from the portal) and a PKR 200 to 500 court fee stamp

## Steps
1. Fill the form and attach the documents.
2. Submit at the DC office counter and collect a receipt.
3. Some districts verify through the local police station or patwari; keep your phone on.
4. Collect the certificate on the date on your receipt.

**Typical time:** 7 to 14 days. If you are under 18, apply on your father's domicile as a dependent.
